# High-Level Guide 2: Tactical Structures, Defensive Blocks & Spatial Dynamics

## 1. Block Heights & Defensive Compactness

Managing block height governs the spaces conceded to the opponent and the team's physical demands:

- **High Block (High Pressing / Full-Pitch Press)**:
  - Defensive line pushed up to midfield (x > 50-60m).
  - Objective: Force inaccurate clearances from goalkeeper and center-backs to recover the ball in the attacking 40 meters.
  - Major Risk: Massive space behind the defensive line; requires a proactive Sweeper-Keeper and high-speed recovery defenders.
- **Mid-Block**:
  - Defensive line positioned between 25m and 40m from own goal.
  - Objective: Close access to central half-spaces and intercept progressive vertical passes. Optimal balance between defensive security and counter-attack threat.
- **Low Block (Deep Box Defense)**:
  - Defensive line set directly in front of the penalty box (< 20m from own goal).
  - Objective: Deny all space in behind, pack the central axis, and force the opponent to cross or settle for low-xG long-distance shots.

---

## 2. Spatial Control, Cover Shadows & Passing Lanes

- **Cover Shadow (Deckungsschatten)**:
  Fundamental pressing technique whereby a defender presses the ball-carrier while angling their body to block the direct passing lane to an opponent positioned behind them. Allows 1 defender to neutralize 2 opponents simultaneously.
- **Third-Man Principle (Third-Man Run)**:
  When Player A cannot pass directly to Player C (blocked by Player B's cover shadow), A plays to intermediate Player B, who lays it off on one touch to surging Player C. The free player is unlocked via an intermediary bounce pass.
- **Overload to Isolate**:
  Deliberate buildup of 4 to 5 players in the left half-space to draw the opponent block, followed by a rapid cross-field switch to an isolated 1v1 winger in open space on the opposite flank.

---

## 3. Rest-Defense Architecture (Preventative Security)

**Rest-Defense** (*Restverteidigung*) refers to the structural positioning of outfield players behind the ball while the team is in active possession:

- **3-2 Structure** (3 deep defenders/fullbacks + 2 central pivots): Guarantees complete coverage across the 3 central channels against direct counter-attacks.
- **2-3 Structure** (2 center-backs + 2 inverted fullbacks stepping into midfield with the anchor): Maximizes central presence and enables immediate counter-pressing upon turnover.
- **Consequence of Faulty Rest-Defense**: If central midfielders advance without defensive cover, ball loss immediately exposes center-backs to 2v2 or 3v2 counter-attacking overloads.

---

## 4. Pressing Traps

Effective pressing is not chasing the ball aimlessly, but funneling the opponent into pre-set trap zones:
- **Pressing Triggers**: Floating horizontal passes, back-to-goal touches, passes to isolated fullbacks pinned against the touchline.
- **Execution**: Once triggered, all surrounding passing options are aggressively suffocated via cover shadows, forcing turnovers or hurried clearances.
